Create an invoice

Get paid in USDC on Arc without running a server. You register the invoice on-chain, share a pay link or QR code, and the payer settles it in one transaction. The USDC goes straight to your wallet and the contract emits a receipt.

1. Network and wallet

The account you connect becomes the merchant: it is paid and it is the only account that can cancel the invoice.

2. Invoice details

Up to 6 decimal places, for example 1250.50.

0 of 140 characters. Only a hash of this text goes on-chain; the text itself travels in the pay link.

Due date

The invoice can be paid up to and including this moment.
